# OVERCLOCK — Roguelite Upgrade System

## Synergies & Trade-Offs
Upgrades are not generic "+10% stats". Every card offers a significant mechanical twist or tactical trade-off.

### Catalog
1. **Cryo Core**: +40% passive cooling, but -15% weapon maximum projectile velocity.
2. **Blood Reactor**: Converting 10 Core HP into an instant 3-second zero-heat Overclock surge when below 25% Core.
3. **Overclock Amplifier**: +3.0s Overclock duration and +50% fire rate, but heat generation rate in Overclock is +50% steeper.
4. **Emergency Thermal Venting**: Taking direct Core damage automatically vents 35% Heat in a damaging radial shockwave.
5. **Railgun Capacitors**: Railgun penetrates infinite targets and leaves an ionized lightning trail, but draws +20 Energy.
6. **Sub-Zero Radiators**: Slaying enemies while in >70% Heat immediately reduces Heat by 8%.
7. **Kinetic Siphon**: Dashing through projectiles absorbs them as Energy and resets 50% Dash cooldown.
8. **Daemon Disruptor**: Hacker interference duration reduced by 80%; Hackers take 200% damage from EMP and Missiles.
9. **Supercritical Stabilizer**: Widens the 95–99% Supercritical Overclock activation window to 90–99%.
