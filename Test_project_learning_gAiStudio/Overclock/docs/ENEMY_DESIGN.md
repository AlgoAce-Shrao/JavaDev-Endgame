# OVERCLOCK — Enemy Roster & Tactical Design

## 1. Drones (Light Patrol)
* **Visual**: Fast, triangular cyan/crimson interceptors.
* **Behavior**: Flock toward the player and fire small plasma pulses. Low health, high numbers.
* **Role**: Primary combo builders and screen pressure.

## 2. Chargers (Heavy Battering Ram)
* **Visual**: Hexagonal armored unit with glowing front ram.
* **Behavior**: Locks onto player position, displays red predictive trajectory line, charges at 4x speed.
* **Counterplay**: Dash perpendicular just before the charge completes.

## 3. Turrets (Fortified Area Denial)
* **Visual**: Octagonal fixed emplacement with rotating dual barrels.
* **Behavior**: Fires circular radial bursts or aimed heavy lasers. High shield armor.
* **Counterplay**: Flank or use Railgun/Missiles with Overclock burst.

## 4. Leeches (Energy Siphon)
* **Visual**: Pulsing diamond entity with energy tethers.
* **Behavior**: Latches an energy drain beam onto the player, starving weapon fire and slowing dash recovery.
* **Counterplay**: Break line of sight or eliminate quickly.

## 5. Hackers (Cyberwarfare Saboteurs)
* **Visual**: Glitching orbital ring craft with holographic disruption waves.
* **Behavior**: Broadcasts interference that glitches UI telemetry, spikes heat by 5%, or inverts minor controls.
* **Counterplay**: Highest target priority.
